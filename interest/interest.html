My Interest - League of Legends

Hero Interest

League of Legends is a 5v5 multiplayer online battle arena(MOBA). Players need to team up to fight the enemy team's Nexus. Players in the game are called Summoners and can choose different champions, earning gold and experience points during matches, which they can use to purchase items and level up.

Besides the normal and ranked matchmaking, it boasts one of the world's largest and most watched esports tournaments. The finals attract millions of viewers to watch online, making it one of the greatest events in the modern competitive gaming world!
