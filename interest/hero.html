My Hero - Spiderman

Hero Interest

Spider-Man is a fictional character created by Stan Lee and Steve Ditko in 1962. He became one of the most well-known superheroes not because of his cool suit or superpowers, but because of his personality and the challenges he faced in life. Peter Parker spent most of his life as a college student from a poor family, struggling to balance his studies, work, and superhero identity, and striving to overcome the trials that come with being Spider-Man and Peter Parker. As readers, we can empathize with him, because even a superhero like Spider-Man inevitably encounters various challenges.
