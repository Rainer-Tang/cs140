Rick Sanchez is an extremely intelligent man who invented the portal gun that makes it possible for him to travel through different dimensions. After spending years away from his home, he returned to live in the same house as his daughter Beth and changed the family's entire life into a sci-fi experience. Rick has a highly cynical outlook on life and an extremely high level of intelligence.

Morty Smith is a 14-year-old high school student who finds himself becoming an assistant to his grandfather Rick against his wishes. Despite being petrified of the frightening concept of the multiverse, Morty demonstrates remarkable bravery and wisdom during difficult situations. His wish is to live a normal life like other teenagers, but regardless of which universe he’s in, he never leaves his grandfather Rick

Beth Smith, Rick's daughter, is a veterinary surgeon who tries her best to keep the family together even under very difficult situations. She is an independent woman and proud of the smartness of her father; at the same time, she is often caught up with her own aspirations and the complicated relationship she shares with Rick and her husband, Jerry.
