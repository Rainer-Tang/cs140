Cheesecake Recipe

Japanese Cheesecake Traditional Cheesecake New York Cheesecake Basque Cheesecake

Ingredients

  • 225g cream cheese
  • 45g unsalted butter
  • 120ml whole milk
  • 6 egg yolks
  • 15ml lemon juice
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 60g cake flour

Melt and mix 225 g cream cheese, 3 tablespoons butter, and 1/2 cup milk until smooth. Then add 6 egg yolks,and 1 tsb into the mixture. Sift in 1/2 cup cake flour and 1 tablespoon cornstarch, and sift once more. Separate 6 cold egg whites and add 1/2 teaspoon cream of tartar, and 1/2 cup sugar and beat until you get soft peaks.

Pour the batter into an 8-inch baking pan lined with parchment paper. Place another large baking dish in the oven. Pour in half a full container of hot water, and carefully place it on the lowest rack of the oven. Bake at 320°F (160°C) for 20 minutes, then lower the temperature to 280°F (140°C), and bake for another 50-60 minutes, or until done. Let the cake rest in the oven with the door half open for 15 minutes, then you can choose to eat it right away or put into fridge for 2 hours for a different texture .
